Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

1. Who We Are

GoVidCo ("GoVidCo," "we," "us," or "our") is a video editing agency operating at govidco.com, founded and operated by Md. Shariful Islam. This policy explains what information we collect from clients, prospective editors, and website visitors, and how we use it.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, primarily through our website forms:

  • Client project inquiries (via the "Get a Quote" form): name, email address, project details, timelines, and links to raw footage you choose to share.
  • Editor applications (via the "Join Our Team" form): name, email address, WhatsApp number, software skills, portfolio links, years of experience, and expected rate.
  • Direct correspondence: information you share with us by email or messaging when discussing a project.

We do not knowingly collect information from visitors under the age of 18, and our services are intended for business use by adults.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to project inquiries and editor applications.
  • To set up and manage a project workspace, match projects with an appropriate editor, and coordinate delivery.
  • To communicate with you about your project or application, including invoices and payment coordination.
  • To improve our services and website.

4. The Identity Firewall — How We Protect Your Information Internally

GoVidCo operates on a strict internal "Identity Firewall": clients and the editors working on their projects do not have direct access to one another's identifying or contact information. Raw project files are shared with editors using a project code rather than your name or company. Only GoVidCo's founder is in direct contact with both clients and editors. This is a business practice designed to protect your privacy and the privacy of the editors working on your project, in addition to the data-handling practices described in this policy.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to deliver our services:

  • With the specific editor assigned to your project, limited to what is necessary to complete the work, and with client-identifying details removed wherever possible.
  • With service providers that support our operations (for example, Google Workspace for email and file storage, and n8n for workflow automation), who process information on our behalf.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of GoVidCo, our clients, or our editors.

6. Data Retention

We retain project-related information for as long as needed to complete the relevant project, maintain business records, and comply with legal or tax obligations. You may request deletion of your information at any time, subject to records we are required to keep for legitimate business or legal purposes.

7. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses a small number of essential and analytics cookies. Essential cookies (for example, remembering that you've dismissed our cookie notice) are required for the site to function and cannot be disabled. We also use privacy-conscious analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand aggregate website traffic — this does not use tracking cookies or collect personally identifiable information. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not sell data to advertisers. You can control cookies through your browser settings at any time.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain uses of it. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold, including restricting access to project files and using reputable third-party providers for email, file storage, and automation. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of our website or services after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information can be sent to admin@govidco.com.
