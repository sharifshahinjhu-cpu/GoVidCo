FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you'd want to know before starting a project with GoVidCo.

How does a project actually work?
You submit your project details and raw footage through our Get a Quote form. We assign a project code and match you with a dedicated editor from our vetted team. You receive deliverables through your project workspace, review them, and request revisions if needed.
What's your typical turnaround time?
Most deliverables are completed within 24–48 hours of us receiving your raw footage and a complete brief, though this varies by project scope, video length, and current workload. We'll confirm a specific timeline when we scope your project.
How many revisions are included?
Each deliverable includes up to two rounds of revisions at no extra charge, as long as the requested changes are consistent with the original brief. Changes beyond that scope may incur additional charges.
How is pricing determined?
Pricing depends on video length, complexity, and volume. Send us your project details and we will send a fixed quote in writing, usually within 24 hours — start here.
Who will be editing my content?
Your project is matched to a senior editor from our vetted team based on your content category. All communication runs through GoVidCo — this keeps quality consistent and your project details handled with care.
What if I'm not happy with the final edit?
Let us know what's not working and we'll revise it within your included revision rounds. Our goal is a deliverable you're genuinely happy to publish.
