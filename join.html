What We Look For

Skills & Experience

Our core work is Shorts/Reels/TikTok and long-form YouTube, with faceless channel systems, podcast edits, ad creatives, and corporate video taken on by request — so editors with any of these specialties are welcome to apply.

A working portfolio or demo reel is required. We care most about pacing, retention-focused editing, and reliability on deadlines.

Shortlisted applicants get a short paid test task — we do not ask anyone to work for free. Pass it and you receive our written editing standard — the same operational brief every editor on the team works to, covering brief intake, revision handling, file naming, and delivery deadlines.
